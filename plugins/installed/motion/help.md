## What it's for
This app adds small motion touches across your whole storefront: a brief fade when a shopper moves between pages, a shimmer over content that is still loading, and a slight press effect on buttons. It runs the same way for every visitor, and turns itself off for anyone whose browser or device asks for reduced motion.

## Good to know
A settings panel offers a motion-intensity choice and a default for reduced motion, but changing either does not currently change anything here; every visitor gets the same effect, and it is only ever turned off by a visitor's own browser or device.
