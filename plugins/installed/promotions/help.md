## What it's for

This app discounts a cart automatically, without the shopper entering anything: a percent or an amount off, free shipping, a free gift, buy X get Y, or buy more and save more. Each promotion can depend on the cart total, the products or categories in the cart (a category counts the categories below it), the shipping country, a customer group, or a customer's first order. It can run between two dates, stop after a number of orders, or apply only with a code. Every order a promotion discounts is recorded.

## Common tasks

- Create a promotion with New promotion on [Promotions](page:index), then choose what the shopper gets and when it applies.
- Open a promotion from the list to change it, or switch it off and on from the list. A promotion that has discounted an order is switched off, never deleted, so its record stays.
- Ask Linda to create, change or switch off a promotion. She shows you the change and waits for your yes.
- See how often each promotion has fired and the most recent orders it discounted, on the same page.

## Good to know

Promotions run in priority order, lowest number first, and they add up. A promotion that doesn't combine applies only on its own: it steps aside when an earlier promotion applied or when the shopper typed a code, and no later promotion applies after it. A promotion unlocked by a code still applies with that code.

A first-order promotion needs a signed-in customer with no order yet; a guest never gets one, because the store has no history to check. A free gift is added to the order at no charge, only while the product is on sale and the item to give is in stock; for a product with several variants, name the variant by its SKU; the cart page doesn't list it yet, the order confirmation does. All discounts together never take more than the cart's value.
