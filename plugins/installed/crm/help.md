## What it's for

This app keeps a pipeline of deals through stages you define, a timeline of every interaction with a lead or customer, and follow-up tasks for your team. A connected mailbox brings email into a shared inbox, and a chat bubble on the storefront lets shoppers message you directly, which staff answer from the dashboard. Linda can also create leads, log interactions, advance deals, and reply to a shopper's chat on your behalf.

## Set it up

Connect a support mailbox — its address and sign-in details — from the [Inbox](page:inbox) page so email replies land in the shared inbox. Two automatic actions can be turned off from [CRM](settings:crm) settings: [](field:crm.auto_create_lead_on_register) and [](field:crm.auto_followup_on_abandoned_cart).

## Common tasks

- Reply to a customer's email from the shared [Inbox](page:inbox).
- Answer a shopper's live chat message from [Support chat](page:chat).
- Move a deal to its next stage on the [Pipeline](page:pipeline) board.
- Check what is due today or overdue on [Tasks](page:tasks).
- Ask Linda to create a lead, log a note, or reply to a support chat.

## Good to know

A lead is created automatically when someone registers, places an order, subscribes to the newsletter, or messages support; a cart left abandoned creates a 24-hour follow-up task. A connected mailbox is checked for new mail every five minutes. Staff are notified the first time a chat thread has an unanswered message waiting.
