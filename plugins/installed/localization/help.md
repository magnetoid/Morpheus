## What it's for

A store offered in more than one language shows its menus and buttons in each of them, but its own content stays in your main language until you translate it. [Translations](page:translations) shows, for each language and kind of content, how much is translated, how many AI drafts wait for you and what is missing, and lets you translate each item beside its original text.

A page in another language appears to shoppers and search engines only once every part of that item's text is translated there and saved. Until then that language's address shows your main-language page and tells search engines so.

## Set it up

The languages your store is offered in are chosen when the store is set up; a store with one language has nothing to translate here. The shop's footer then offers a language switcher.

Tick "Language bar" at the top of [Translations](page:translations) and a visitor whose browser prefers another of your languages sees "This page is also in …", with Switch and Stay. Their choice is remembered for a year. It is off until you turn it on.

## Common tasks

- Open an item, write its translation beside the original and save. Emptying a field removes its translation.
- Translate an experience in two places: its name and descriptions under Products, its details (duration, meeting point, terms, highlights, the itinerary, guest questions) under Experience details.
- In a list, write one item per line. In an itinerary or the guest questions, put each step or question on its own line with its detail or answer below it, and a blank line between them.
- Click "Draft with AI" on an item, or "Draft the missing ones with AI" for up to 50 at a time, then read, correct and save each draft. Shoppers never see a draft you have not saved.
- Ask Linda how much is translated, to draft the missing ones, or to publish drafts you read.

## Good to know

AI drafting uses your store's AI provider and obeys the switch and daily spending limit in your AI settings. A draft never replaces a translation you saved. A page written in another language (a Serbian article) is not offered for translation: it appears in its own language only. A saved AI draft is marked as written with AI for search engines (EU AI Act Article 50); change its text and save, and the mark goes.
