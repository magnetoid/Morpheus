## What it's for

A store offered in more than one language shows its menus and buttons in each of them, but its products, experiences, places, hotels, events and pages stay in your main language until you translate them. [Translations](page:translations) shows, for each language and kind of content, how much is translated, how many AI drafts wait for you, and what is missing, and lets you translate each item beside its original text.

A page in another language appears to shoppers and search engines only once every part of that item's text that has words in your main language is translated there and saved. Until then the other language's address shows your main-language page and tells search engines so, and no page claims a language it is not written in.

## Set it up

The languages your store is offered in are chosen when the store is set up; a store with one language has nothing to translate here. The footer of the shop offers a language switcher whenever there is more than one.

Tick "Language bar" at the top of [Translations](page:translations) and a visitor whose browser prefers another language your store has sees a bar at the top of the page: "This page is also in …", with Switch and Stay. Their choice is remembered for a year. It is off until you turn it on.

## Common tasks

- Open an item, write its translation beside the original and save. Emptying a field removes its translation.
- Click "Draft with AI" on an item, or "Draft the missing ones with AI" for up to 50 at a time, then read each draft, correct it and save it. A draft is never shown to shoppers until you save it.
- Ask Linda how much is translated, to draft the missing items, or to publish drafts you have read.

## Good to know

AI drafting uses your store's AI provider and obeys the switch and the daily spending limit in your AI settings; when either stops it, nothing is written and the page says why. A draft never replaces a translation you saved. Once you save or publish a draft it is your text, reviewed and published by you, so it carries no "written with AI" label on the shop; the record still notes that an AI wrote the first version.
