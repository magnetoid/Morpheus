## What it's for

This app adds a language switcher to the storefront footer, so a shopper can pick one of the languages your store actually offers. Those languages are set when your store is deployed, not from this app's own pages.

## Good to know

The [Languages](page:languages) page lets you type a list of languages, but saving it does not change which languages your storefront offers, the switcher's options, or anything else — it has no effect today. The [Translations & languages](page:translations) page is meant to list translated content, but it currently always shows as empty, whether or not any translations exist.
