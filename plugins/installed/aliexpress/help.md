## What it's for

This app connects your store to AliExpress through its official dropshipping program. Once connected, staff can search AliExpress's catalogue from the dashboard, bring a product in as a draft to review and price, and let the store place and track supplier orders on its own.

## Set it up

Register a "Drop Shipping" developer app at https://openservice.aliexpress.com, then enter its AppKey and AppSecret, plus your shipping destination and a price markup, on [AliExpress dropshipping](settings:aliexpress). Connect your AliExpress buyer account from the [AliExpress](page:orders) page — it must already belong to AliExpress's Dropshipping Program.

## Common tasks

- Connect or disconnect the AliExpress account.
- Search AliExpress and import a product as a draft, then review its price before publishing it.
- Link an existing product to an AliExpress item on its edit page.
- Send a paid order to AliExpress by hand if it was not sent automatically, or retry one that failed.
- Refresh orders now to pick up payment and tracking updates early.

## Good to know

A paid order is sent to AliExpress on its own, and the store ships it and emails the customer only once every AliExpress order it created has a tracking number — an order split across sellers waits for all of them. Prices are quoted directly in your store's currency by AliExpress. There is no way to cancel an order at AliExpress from here: a store cancellation is only noted for staff to cancel it by hand.
