## What it's for

Nothing in the store currently uses this app. Its database can hold a quiz — a title and introduction, a list of multiple-choice or text questions, and a mapping from answer patterns to a result category — meant to end on a personalised category of products, with the shopper's answers saved for later. Today there is no page where a shopper takes a quiz, no way for an answer to be saved, and no dashboard screen for writing one.

## Good to know

Its settings panel, [](settings:discovery_quiz), has one switch for requiring consent before saving answers, but nothing in the store reads it yet. A small piece of storefront code for showing a shopper their quiz result also exists, but nothing ever gives it a result to show, so it never displays anything.
