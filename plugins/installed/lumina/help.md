## What it's for

This app adds a "Write a book" page, linked from the storefront footer, that promotes the Lumina Book Creator — a separate tool for writing a book with AI and voice, translating it into other languages, and selling it on your store. Its "Start creating" buttons send a visitor to that tool.

## Set it up

[](field:lumina.app_url) in [](settings:lumina) sets where the page's buttons send a visitor. Left blank, they point at the original Lumina app at https://lumina.dotbooks.store, so set your own address here if you run a different one.

## Good to know

The page is public — no sign-in is needed to view it, and it renders inside your own storefront theme like any other page. Turning this app off removes the page and its footer link.
