## What it's for

Nothing in the store currently uses this app. Its database can hold a long-form post built from blocks — text, an image, a video, a linked product, a linked collection — meant to render on its own storefront page. The journal pages shoppers can actually see, linked from navigation and listed in search, are a separate, already-working feature and do not use this app at all.

## Good to know

Its settings panel has one switch for turning this app's journal on, but nothing reads it. The template that would render one of its posts is wired to a storefront placement nothing ever shows, so even a saved post would not appear anywhere.
