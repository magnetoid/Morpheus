## What it's for
This app is meant to add photos and videos to customer reviews, show the approved ones on a product's photo gallery and as quoted highlights above its description, and invite customers into a creator programme some months after delivery.

## Good to know
None of this currently shows on your storefront: there is no screen to add or approve a customer's photo or video, so a product page never receives any to show. This app has no settings of its own.
