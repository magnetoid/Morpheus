## What it's for

This app collects events that need a staff member's attention — a return request coming in, a product running low or going out of stock, an automatic task failing, or another app deciding something is worth a look — and puts them in one place: the bell icon at the top of every dashboard page, and the full [Notifications](page:all) list. Every signed-in staff member sees their own notifications and their own unread count.

## Common tasks

- Open the bell to see your latest notifications and jump straight to what they're about.
- Open [Notifications](page:all) for the full list, or to see only what's unread.
- Mark one notification, or everything, as read.

## Good to know

Right now, a notification goes to every staff member at once — there is no way yet to choose which kinds of notification you personally want to see.
