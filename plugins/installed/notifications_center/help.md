## What it's for

This app collects events that need a staff member's attention — a return request coming in, a product running low or going out of stock, an automatic task failing, or another app deciding something is worth a look — and puts them in one place: the bell icon at the top of every dashboard page, and the full [Notifications](page:all) list. Every signed-in staff member has their own notifications and their own unread count.

## Common tasks

- Open the bell to see your latest notifications. Click one to open what it is about; hover it to mark it read, snooze it until tomorrow, archive it or stop getting that kind.
- Open [Notifications](page:all) for everything, sorted into Inbox, Unread, Snoozed and Archived, and filter by level or kind.
- Snooze a notification for an hour, until tomorrow morning or for a week. It comes back to the top of your inbox, unread, when the time is up.
- Choose which kinds of notification you get in [Notification settings](page:preferences). Your choice is yours alone.

## Good to know

- Repeats stack: the same product running low again, or the same automatic task failing again, adds to the notification you haven't read yet instead of adding a new one. The count shows how many times it happened, and the notification shows the most recent ones. Once you read or archive it, the next repeat starts a new one.
- Each notification has a level. Warnings have an amber edge, urgent ones a red edge, and the bell's counter turns red while something urgent is unread. Out of stock is urgent; low stock, return requests and failed automatic tasks are warnings.
- The bell's counter refreshes by itself every 30 seconds while the dashboard is open.
- Archiving keeps a notification; it only leaves the inbox. Snoozed notifications don't count as unread until they come back.
- Linda can list your notifications and, with your yes, mark, snooze, archive or mute them for you.
