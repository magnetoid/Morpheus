## What it's for

After an order is placed, this app shows one extra product on the confirmation page, with a line of your own wording above it, as a simple "you might also like" suggestion.

## Set it up

Nothing is required — it is on by default and falls back to your newest active product that the customer did not just buy. To choose a specific product instead, type its slug and your own line of copy into [Post-checkout upsell](settings:post_checkout_upsell).

## Good to know

This only suggests a product with a link to its page — it does not add anything to the order the customer just placed, and buying the suggestion is an ordinary new purchase. If the product slug you typed in no longer matches a real, active product, the suggestion quietly falls back to your newest one instead, with nothing to tell you that happened.
