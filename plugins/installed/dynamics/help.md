## What it's for

This app chooses which products show in recommendation blocks around your storefront — home page, product pages, cart, checkout and the order confirmation — and can also take over product ordering on surfaces your theme already has, such as the home hero or the shop's default product list. Each block can run a simple rule (recently viewed, related, on sale, best sellers, a manual filter…) or a self-learning "Autopilot" strategy that adjusts to what shoppers actually view and buy. A nightly process can also propose merchandising changes for you to approve.

## Set it up

Open [](page:index) to add a block to a slot, or click into a surface to take control of it with one click. [](field:dynamics.auto_apply) in [](settings:dynamics) turns Autopilot's low-risk proposals into automatic changes; left off, every proposal waits for you in [](page:proposals).

## Common tasks

- Add a block, for example "frequently bought together", to a page slot and choose how many products it shows.
- Take control of a theme surface, like the home hero or a category page's order, with a self-learning block.
- Preview a block to see which products it would pick and why, including by visitor segment for Autopilot blocks.
- Review, then approve or dismiss, the merchandising changes Autopilot proposes in [](page:proposals).

## Good to know

A block only shows once it is enabled and placed on a slot your theme actually renders, or on a surface you have taken control of. Autopilot blocks improve as more shoppers view and buy, and start from sensible defaults before any data comes in. Disabling this app removes every block, every surface takeover and its settings page; nothing it added is left behind.
