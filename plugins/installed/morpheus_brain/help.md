## What it's for
This is a read-only overview of how the store's code and content are doing, built from signals the platform already produces: installed apps and their versions, code-quality findings, recent server errors, SEO and content health, and storefront-specific signals. A separate tab holds a longer AI-written assessment of the whole store.

## Set it up
There is nothing to switch on in [](page:brain). Most of it works without any AI connected; the long-form write-up needs an AI provider configured in Settings → AI — without one it says so instead of showing a briefing.

## Common tasks
- Open the overview tab for a first look at code, content and store health.
- Read code-quality findings and server error counts on their own tabs.
- Check SEO and content gaps, and storefront-specific signals, on their tabs.
- Read suggested improvements, grouped by code, features and store growth.
- Click "Refresh analysis" to re-run the assessment behind those suggestions.
- Click "Regenerate briefing" on the advisory tab for a fresh long-form write-up.

## Good to know
This page only reads existing data; nothing here changes the store on its own. A "Daily Reports" tab is present but nothing currently fills it with new entries, so do not expect fresh ones there. Every other section degrades gracefully: a source that is switched off or has nothing to show simply says so instead of breaking the page.
