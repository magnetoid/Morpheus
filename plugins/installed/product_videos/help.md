## What it's for
This app shows one or more videos on a product's page, just above its long description. Paste a YouTube or Vimeo link and it embeds automatically; paste a direct video file link and it plays in a plain video player.

## Set it up
Add a video on the product's own edit page: paste a YouTube, Vimeo or direct video link (or, for anything unusual, raw embed code) into the video box in its photos section. Open a video's tile to change its details, or use the X on the tile to remove it.

## Good to know
A video only shows on the product page once it is marked active, in the order you set. Raw embed code is restricted to known video hosts for safety; anything that fails that check is dropped, and the link-based embed is used instead. Turning this app off removes the video section from every product page.
