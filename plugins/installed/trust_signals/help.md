summary: Shows a star rating, a verified-buyer share and a recent-purchases ticker on a product's page.

## What it's for
This app shows up to three trust signals on a product's page, just under the price: its star rating and review count, the share of its reviews left by verified buyers, and a ticker such as "12 ordered in the last 3 days." Each one only shows once there is enough real data behind it.

## Set it up
[](settings:trust_signals) lists this app among your settings, but its screen currently shows no fields to fill in, so every store runs on the same built-in defaults: all three signals on, a star rating needs at least 3 reviews, the verified-buyer share needs at least 5 reviews with most of them verified, and the ticker needs at least 3 orders in the last 72 hours.

## Good to know
Each signal hides itself rather than show a weak number, using those same built-in thresholds — the verified-buyer share only shows above 50% verified. Numbers are refreshed every few minutes, so a brand new review or order can take a short while to show.
