## What it's for

Nothing in the store currently uses this app. Its database can hold a scheduled release of a product (a "drop"), a queue or raffle position for a shopper waiting on it, and a waitlist of shoppers to notify when a sold-out product is back. Today there is no dashboard screen for scheduling a drop, no page where a shopper joins a queue or a raffle, and nothing that sends a notification when a waitlisted product returns.

## Good to know

A small piece of storefront code for showing a drop's countdown, a "live now" notice, or a waitlist sign-up form exists on the product page, but nothing ever gives it a drop to show, so it never displays anything.
