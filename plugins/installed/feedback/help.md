## What it's for

This app gives every staff member a quick way to report something wrong while they are using the dashboard. "Send feedback," in the menu under your account at the top right, opens a short form that takes their message, offers to capture a picture of their screen, and automatically attaches the page they were on and any recent errors the browser or the server had already logged. Each report becomes a ticket under [Feedback](page:tickets) for you to read and work through.

## Common tasks

- Send feedback on something wrong from the account menu, top right of any page.
- Read incoming tickets and the evidence attached to each one.
- Mark a ticket in progress or closed once it's handled.

## Good to know

Any signed-in staff member can send feedback, even one without permission to see the ticket queue — reporting a problem should never need special permission. The screenshot is optional: a reporter can decline their browser's share prompt and still send their report.
