## What it's for

This app turns part of your catalogue into bookable experiences, stays, and local goods offered by hosts. Shoppers browse and search listings, hosts manage their own availability and prices, and staff see every booking and enquiry from the dashboard. It also publishes destination guides, hotel listings, and an events calendar.

## Set it up

This app ships off until a store turns it on. Whether prices show, and whether a submission can hold a confirmed booking rather than only create an enquiry, are set outside the dashboard.

## Common tasks

- See and manage every [listing](page:listings): experiences, stays, and local goods, from any host.
- See [bookings](page:bookings) and reply to [enquiries](page:enquiries).
- See [stays](page:stays) at hotels and guesthouses.
- See enquiries, bookings, their value, how far ahead guests ask, and the top listings and regions on [Conversions](page:conversions).
- Ask Linda to find a listing, change its details or report conversions; a change still needs your yes.

## Good to know

This app never takes payment itself: a submission becomes an enquiry for the host to follow up or, where confirmed bookings are switched on, a hold on inventory with nothing charged. A review only counts toward a listing's rating once it comes from a real, confirmed booking.

Each enquiry and booking counts once: in Analytics as a lead or a booking, never a sale; in Google Analytics, when connected, as a lead, or a purchase once confirmed. A search by region, date, category or group size alone is a search too.

The country comes from the theme (Montenegro or Croatia): a listing's region is picked from its regions, and every page that names the country follows it.

Each region's page shows its geography, experiences, hotels, places and upcoming events, and links to each activity there; search engines are sent to those pages rather than to a filtered search.

A hotel can be listed without rooms or rates: its card says "Contact for price" and its page sends you the guest's enquiry. Seeded photos come from Wikimedia Commons and name their photographer and licence; one you upload replaces the photo and its credit.

Where the theme includes one, an investor page, shared by link and kept out of search results, shows public market figures and what the site lists today; never bookings, enquiries or revenue.

Search engines see an experience as available only while it has an open date and the store takes bookings.
