## What it's for

This app turns part of your catalogue into bookable experiences, stays, and local goods offered by hosts. Shoppers browse and search listings, hosts manage their own availability and prices, and staff see every booking and enquiry from the dashboard. It also publishes destination guides, hotel listings, and an events calendar built around the same hosts.

## Set it up

This app ships off until a store turns it on. Once on, a few important behaviours — whether prices are shown, and whether a submission can hold a confirmed booking rather than only create an enquiry — are set outside the dashboard, not on a settings screen here. Each listing needs a host to belong to.

## Common tasks

- See and manage every [listing](page:listings): experiences, stays, and local goods, from any host.
- See [bookings](page:bookings) and reply to [enquiries](page:enquiries).
- See [stays](page:stays) at hotels and guesthouses.
- Open and edit any host's listing on their behalf.
- Ask Linda to find a listing or change its details; a change still needs your yes.

## Good to know

However it is configured, this app never takes payment itself: a submission either becomes an enquiry — a lead for the host to follow up — or, where confirmed bookings are switched on, a hold on inventory with nothing charged. Any money changes hands outside the store, arranged by the host or the merchant. A review only counts toward a listing's rating once it comes from a real, confirmed booking.
