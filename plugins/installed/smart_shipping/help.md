## What it's for

This app shows a second list of shipping options on the checkout page, alongside your store's own shipping-choice list. It has one setting, [](field:smart_shipping.show_lowest_carbon_badge), meant to flag the lowest-emission option.

## Good to know

Picking an option from this app's list at checkout does not change what a shopper is charged or which shipping method is used on the order — the choice that counts is the one in your theme's own checkout form. Turning [](field:smart_shipping.show_lowest_carbon_badge) on or off has no effect; the badge never appears either way. Nothing in this app currently records a carrier rate or a carbon estimate. Your store's actual shipping zones, rates and carrier rates come from the [Shipping](app:shipping) app.
