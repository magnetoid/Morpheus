## What it's for

This app keeps track of how much stock each product has, per warehouse. It automatically holds stock when a shopper checks out, removes it once the order is paid, and puts it back if the order is cancelled or a return is refunded. It can also email a shopper once a sold-out product is back in stock, and it forecasts which products are about to run out or are overstocked.

## Common tasks

- Open the [Stockout forecast](page:stockout-forecast) report — linked from a card on the [Products](section:products) area — to see which products are projected to run out soon, and which look overstocked.
- Ask Linda for a low-stock report, to adjust how much stock a product has, or to schedule a price change for a future date.

## Good to know

There is currently no dashboard page for entering or changing a stock quantity directly — today that's done by asking Linda, or over the store's API. "Email me when it's back" subscriptions are stored on the product page and emailed automatically once stock is added back; nothing needs to be turned on for this.
