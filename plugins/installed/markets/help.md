## What it's for

This app lets you define markets — groups of countries with their own currency and locale — and it automatically works out which market a visitor is browsing from, by their country. Store staff use the Markets page to add, edit and remove markets.

## Common tasks

- Add a market with its countries, currency and locale from the [Markets](page:index) page.
- Mark one market as the default, used for any visitor whose country doesn't match another market.
- Switch a market off without deleting it.
- Ask Linda to list your configured markets.

## Good to know

Apart from telling browsers which language a page is in, a market changes nothing a shopper sees today. The price-adjustment percentage and the per-product prices you can set on a market do not currently change any price a shopper sees or is charged — prices are the same in every market today.
