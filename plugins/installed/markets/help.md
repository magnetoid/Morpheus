## What it's for

This app lets you define markets: groups of countries with their own currency and locale. For each market it works out what every product would cost in that market's currency, from your store price, an optional percentage adjustment, the exchange rate and the rounding you choose. Store staff use the Markets page to add, edit and remove markets and to check the prices.

## Set it up

Add a market from the [Markets](page:index) page: a name, a code, its countries, a currency and a locale. Choose how converted prices are rounded: exact to the cent, to a whole number, or ending in .99. A market in a different currency from your store needs an exchange rate before you can switch it on. The rates come from the European Central Bank every day; to use only your own, change where the rates come from in the app's settings.

## Common tasks

- Open a market to see its exchange rate, its source and age, and a ten-product price preview.
- Type your own rate for a currency and save it. The daily fetch never replaces a rate you typed; empty the box and save to go back to the daily rate. Without a daily rate to fall back on, your rate stays while an active market uses it.
- Press Fetch rates now to update the rates at once (not offered when rates are typed by hand).
- A currency with no rate yet: save the new market switched off, set its rate on its page, then switch it on.
- Mark one market as the default, used for any visitor whose country doesn't match another market.
- Switch a market off without deleting it.
- Ask Linda to show markets and rates, change a market, fix a product's price in one or set a rate; she asks for your yes first.

## Good to know

A price you type for a product in a market, in the product's own list of prices, wins over the conversion, and a rate that is more than three days old is flagged as out of date. Shoppers still see your store currency: showing and charging a market's prices comes in a later update, so a market changes nothing a shopper sees yet apart from the language the page declares. The percentage adjustment and fixed prices apply to the preview now and to shoppers later.
