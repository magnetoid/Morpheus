## What it's for

This app captures email addresses with popups on the storefront and keeps a confirmed, double-opt-in subscriber list. It also sends a win-back email to a subscribed customer who slips into the "at risk" group, at most once a month. Linda can report subscriber counts and create or toggle a popup.

## Set it up

Create and turn on a signup popup from [Popups](page:popups) — nothing shows on the storefront until one is active. Win-back email is on by default; turn it off, or add a coupon code to include, from [Newsletter](settings:newsletter) settings: [](field:newsletter.winback_enabled), [](field:newsletter.winback_coupon_code).

## Common tasks

- See, filter, and export your subscriber list from [Subscribers](page:subscribers).
- Create and turn on a signup popup from [Popups](page:popups).
- Send a campaign, or a test copy of it, to confirmed subscribers from [Campaigns](page:newsletter-campaigns).
- Ask Linda for subscriber counts, or to create or toggle a popup.

## Good to know

A subscriber must confirm their email before they count as subscribed or receive anything else. There is currently no screen to write a new campaign's subject and content — the Campaigns page can only send a campaign that already exists.
