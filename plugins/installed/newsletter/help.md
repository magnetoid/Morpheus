## What it's for

This app captures email addresses with popups on the storefront and keeps a confirmed, double-opt-in subscriber list. You write campaigns here and send them now or at a set time, to every confirmed subscriber or only those in one customer group (champions, loyal, potential, new, at risk, lost). It also sends a win-back email to a subscribed customer who slips into the "at risk" group, at most once a month. Linda can report subscriber counts, create or toggle a popup, and list, draft and send campaigns.

## Set it up

Create and turn on a signup popup from [Popups](page:popups) — nothing shows on the storefront until one is active. Win-back email is on by default; turn it off, or add a coupon code to include, from [Newsletter](settings:newsletter) settings: [](field:newsletter.winback_enabled), [](field:newsletter.winback_coupon_code). To ask at checkout too, switch on [](field:newsletter.checkout_opt_in): an unticked box, and a shopper who ticks it gets the confirmation email like any other signup.

## Common tasks

- See, filter, and export your subscriber list from [Subscribers](page:subscribers).
- Create and turn on a signup popup from [Popups](page:popups).
- Write a campaign from [Campaigns](page:newsletter-campaigns) with New campaign: a name only you see, a subject, the line inboxes show after it, the message and the audience. Save it as a draft, schedule it for a time on your store's clock, or send it now.
- Send yourself a test copy of a saved campaign before it goes out.
- Cancel a scheduled campaign to make it a draft you can edit again.
- Ask Linda for subscriber counts, to create or toggle a popup, or to draft, schedule or send a campaign. She asks for your yes before she drafts or sends one.

## Good to know

A subscriber must confirm their email before they count as subscribed or receive anything else. A customer group only narrows the confirmed list: a customer who never subscribed gets no campaign. Every campaign goes out in your store's email look with a one-click unsubscribe, and anything unsafe in the message is removed. A scheduled campaign that could not go out within a day of its time is not sent: it goes back to draft and the list says it missed its send time. Groups need the Customers app, which works them out every night.
