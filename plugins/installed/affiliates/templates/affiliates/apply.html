{% comment %}── FAQ ──────────────────────────────────────────────{% endcomment %}

Common questions

How long does the cookie last?

30 days. A reader who clicks your link and buys anything within that window is attributed to you, even if they leave and come back later.

Can I link to specific books or only the storefront?

Either. You can generate per-book links from your dashboard, or use your handle on any URL — the dashboard tracks all of them.

Is there an exclusivity clause?

No. Recommend whoever you want, however you want. We'd just rather not be one of seventy logos on a deals page.

When do you pay out?

Monthly, on the 1st. Under $25 carries over to the next cycle. Payment goes to the email you set on your profile, via PayPal, wire, or store credit (we add 10% if you pick credit).

What about returns and refunds?

A refund inside 30 days reverses the commission on that order. We try to keep refund rate transparent on your dashboard so you can see what's actually keeping.

Why might my application get rejected?

Mostly: empty/spammy profiles, deals-and-coupons aggregators, and bot-driven traffic. If we say no, we'll usually tell you why and you can re-apply.
