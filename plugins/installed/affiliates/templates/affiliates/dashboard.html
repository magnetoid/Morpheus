How affiliate works · payouts · the math

How a click becomes commission. A reader clicks your tracked link, lands on dot books, and a 30-day cookie is set. If they buy any book in that window — even a different one than they clicked through to — the order is attributed to you and the program's commission rate is applied to the subtotal.

EPC (Earnings per Click) is the headline number — your accrued commission divided by your clicks over the last 30 days. A good EPC for a literary affiliate sits around $0.20–$0.60. Below $0.05 means either your audience isn't a fit or your landing pages need work.

CR (Conversion Rate) is what percentage of clicks turn into orders. Industry ecommerce average is 2–4%; literary niches with engaged audiences often hit 5–8%.

Payouts are issued monthly once your accrued balance crosses the program's payout threshold (usually $25). We pay via the method on file in your profile.

Approved vs pending conversions: an order is pending until the return window closes (14 days for physical books, immediate for digital). Pending conversions count toward your visible metrics but only approved ones are paid.
