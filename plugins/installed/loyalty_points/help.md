## What it's for

Signed-in customers earn points on every paid order — one point per unit of store currency by default — and can see their balance and what it is worth on their account page. At checkout, a shopper can choose to spend some or all of their points for a discount on that order.

## Set it up

From [Loyalty points](settings:loyalty_points) settings, set [](field:loyalty_points.redemption_rate) — how many points equal one unit of store currency — and, if you want a limit, [](field:loyalty_points.max_redeem_fraction), the share of an order's total that points are allowed to cover.

## Common tasks

- Change how many points equal one unit of store currency.
- Limit how much of an order points are allowed to pay for.
- Ask Linda for a customer's points balance, or to adjust it by hand.

## Good to know

Only signed-in customers earn points — a guest checkout earns nothing. Cancelling an order gives back any points it spent; a refund returns a share of the points matching the refunded amount.
