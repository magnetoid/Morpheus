## What it's for
This app measures how much the rest of the store's apps actually get used — both by staff in the dashboard and by Linda's tools — and turns that into one install health score out of 100, plus a table of which apps are used and which have gone untouched. It's for seeing what's actually in use versus what was turned on and forgotten.

## Set it up
Nothing to configure. It starts counting automatically once it's on — there are no settings.

## Common tasks
- Open [Feature adoption](page:adoption) from the card on the [Analytics](section:analytics) section to see the full report
- Check the install health score and its three parts: breadth of apps used, Linda tool-call volume, and how recently the dashboard was opened
- Look at the "Never used in 90 days" list to find apps that are turned on but not being used
- Compare an app's use over the last 7, 30 and 90 days

## Good to know
Usage is counted only as a daily total per app and surface — no visitor data, no individual actions, nothing personal is recorded. Counts are collected continuously but only written to the report once an hour, so very recent activity may not show yet. The health score gives up to 40 points for the share of your enabled apps that saw any use in the last 30 days, up to 30 for Linda tool-call volume (capped at 100 calls), and up to 30 for how recently someone opened the dashboard, fading to zero after 14 days with no visit.
