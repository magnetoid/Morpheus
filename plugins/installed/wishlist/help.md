## What it's for
This app gives each shopper, signed in or not, a wishlist they can add products to from a button on the product page, and a page to view and manage it. A shopper can turn their wishlist into a public, read-only link to share — for a gift list, for example. Linda can also look up what's in a shopper's wishlist, or add a product to it, when asked in a conversation.

## Common tasks
- Ask Linda what is in a shopper's wishlist, or to add a product to it.

## Good to know
This app has no settings and no page of its own in the dashboard; shoppers manage their own wishlist from the storefront. A guest's wishlist follows their browser session until they sign in. Erasing a customer's data under a privacy request also deletes their wishlist and any shared link to it.
