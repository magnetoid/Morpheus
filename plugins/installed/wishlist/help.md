---

## What it's for
This app gives each shopper, signed in or not, a wishlist they can add products to from a button on the product page, and a page on the storefront to view and manage it. A shopper can turn their wishlist into a public, read-only link to share, for a gift list for example. Everything here is done by the shopper; staff have nothing to set up or manage.

## Good to know
This app has no settings and no page of its own in the dashboard, and Linda cannot read or change a shopper's wishlist for you. A guest's wishlist follows their browser session until they sign in. Erasing a customer's data under a privacy request also deletes their wishlist and any shared link to it.
