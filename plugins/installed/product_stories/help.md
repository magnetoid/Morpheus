## What it's for

This app adds a short row of panels to a product page — each with an image, a heading and a line or two of body text — that a shopper scrolls through, making the case for why they'll love the product. Staff write and reorder these panels per product.

## Common tasks

- Open the [Stories](page:stories) page, pick a product, and add, edit, reorder or remove its panels.
- Ask Linda to list or add a panel to a product's story.

## Good to know

Only a panel marked active shows on the storefront; an inactive one is saved but stays hidden.
