## What it's for

This app shows shoppers a cookie-consent banner the first time they visit, with separate choices for analytics, marketing and other optional cookies — the "necessary" cookies that run the store are never optional. Their choice is remembered for a year and logged, so you can show that a shopper agreed if you are ever asked to. A footer link lets a shopper change their mind later. Marketing pixels, the analytics beacon, and the conversion data sent to ad platforms for an order all check this choice first.

## Set it up

This app has no settings of its own. The banner, and everything it gates, only run while GDPR/ePrivacy features are switched on for the store, in [](settings:gdpr).

## Good to know

A shopper's browser can send a "do not track" signal (Global Privacy Control); the banner treats that the same as refusing everything but necessary cookies, though the shopper can still open the preferences page and opt in anyway. Turning GDPR features off for the store, or turning this app off, stops the banner and lets every tag load for every visitor, with nothing held back.
