## What it's for

This app calculates tax on orders from the regions and rates you set up — flat-rate VAT, US-style sales tax, or a rate just for one product category. Store staff manage regions and rates; the storefront applies the right one automatically based on the shopper's address.

## Set it up

Add at least one [tax region](page:regions) and give it a rate before tax can be calculated. Pick a default region for orders whose address doesn't match any region you've set up, or turn tax off entirely from the same page.

## Common tasks

- Add a tax region for a country or state and set its rate from the [Tax regions](page:regions) page.
- Add a lower or zero rate for one product category within a region, such as tax-exempt books.
- Change the default region used when an order's address doesn't match a specific region.
- Ask Linda to list your current tax rates or set a new one for a region.

## Good to know

Tax is calculated from each order's line items at checkout, using the most specific rate that matches: a category rate first, then the region's standard rate. Setting a region's calculation to "No tax" turns off tax for orders in that region.
