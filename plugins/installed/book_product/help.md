## What it's for

This app turns a regular product into a book. It adds book fields — author, series, format, page count, language and more — to the product page's Book details card, and it builds browsable pages for genres, topics, authors, publishers, series and imprints. Shoppers can filter the all-books page by genre, topic, author, format or length. A book card names the book's formats (Print · PDF); a PDF or audiobook you give away (its variant priced 0) shows as Free PDF or Free audiobook beside the printed price. As a shopper types in the search box, matching genres and authors appear above the books. If a book has a cover, its product page shows it on an interactive 3D book, and if it has more than one language edition linked together, shoppers can switch between them.

## Common tasks

- Fill in a book's author, series, format, page count and other details from the product page's Book details card, and drop its cover there, the front alone or the whole wrap (back, spine and front): a PDF, PNG or JPG of up to 50 MB. Its width, height and spine split a wrap exactly.
- Open the [Book taxonomies](page:book-taxonomies) page to see and manage every genre, topic, author, publisher, series and imprint.
- Write or regenerate the introduction and search description for one of those pages, one at a time or in bulk.
- Give a taxonomy page its own image, and see each book's author and genres in the product list.
- Switch on [Social cards for books](field:book_product.social_cards) so a shared book page shows a card with the whole cover, the title, the author and the start of the book's description. Its wording and colours are in the same [Books settings](settings:book_product).

## Good to know

Turning this app off removes the Book details card from the product page and takes down the author, genre, series and other book pages. The audiobook app and the carbon-footprint badge both depend on this one. A book's card is drawn the first time its page is opened after a change, and Linda can draw one when you ask. A share image you picked for the page wins over the card, and switching the cards off shares the cover again at once. Review any text this app writes before you publish it: treat it as a starting draft.
