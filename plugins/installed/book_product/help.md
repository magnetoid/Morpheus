## What it's for

This app turns a regular product into a book. It adds book fields — author, series, format, page count, language and more — to the product page's Book details card, and it builds browsable pages for genres, topics, authors, publishers, series and imprints. Shoppers can filter the all-books page by genre, topic, author, format or length. If a book has a cover file, its product page shows an interactive 3D preview of the cover, and if it has more than one language edition linked together, shoppers can switch between them.

## Common tasks

- Fill in a book's author, series, format, page count and other details from the product page's Book details card.
- Open the [Book taxonomies](page:book-taxonomies) page to see and manage every genre, topic, author, publisher, series and imprint.
- Write or regenerate the introduction and search description for one of those pages, one at a time or in bulk.
- Give a taxonomy page its own image so it reads well when a shopper lands on it.

## Good to know

Turning this app off removes the Book details card from the product page and takes down the author, genre, series and other book pages. The audiobook app and the carbon-footprint badge both depend on this one. Review any text this app writes for you before you publish it — treat it as a starting draft, not final copy.
