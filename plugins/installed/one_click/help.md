## What it's for

This app is meant to let a customer who already placed one order come back and check out again with a single tap, instead of filling in the cart form a second time, by remembering their device from that first order.

## Set it up

Dashboard → Apps can switch this app on. [Its settings](settings:one_click) list only one toggle, and nothing today reads it either.

## Good to know

None of this works yet. No email is sent after a first order, no device is ever remembered, and the one-tap button this app was meant to add to the cart never appears on any page, because the condition behind it is never met. Turning the app on or off makes no visible difference to a shopper.
