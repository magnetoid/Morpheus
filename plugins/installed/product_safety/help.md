## What it's for

The EU's General Product Safety Regulation asks every online listing of goods sold to shoppers in the EU to name the manufacturer (name, postal address and an email or website), a responsible person in the EU when the manufacturer is outside it, and the product's warnings and safety information. This app shows those details in a "Product safety information" section under the description on the product page, and names the manufacturer in the page's data for search engines. Product codes such as GTIN, MPN or ISBN are entered in the product's identifier fields, which the page already shows.

## Set it up

If your store makes or brands what it sells, or answers for it in the EU, fill in your own details once in [Settings](settings:product_safety): [](field:product_safety.manufacturer_name) and the address and contact beside it, and [](field:product_safety.responsible_name) with theirs. Every product sold as goods that names no manufacturer or responsible person of its own then shows these. Services and downloads never do.

## Common tasks

- Give one product its own manufacturer, responsible person, warnings or a link to its instructions in the Product safety card of the product editor in [](section:products). Emptying a field there puts the store's details back.
- Ask Linda what a product's page shows, or to fill in its safety details; she asks before saving.

## Good to know

A manufacturer comes whole from one place: a product that fills in any of its manufacturer fields shows only its own, never your store's address beside it. The same goes for the responsible person. A contact becomes a link only when it is an email address or a web address. Warnings are shown as you write them, so write them in your shoppers' language.
