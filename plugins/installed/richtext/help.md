## What it's for

This app provides the text editor you type into when you write a product description, a category description, or a content page — the box with a toolbar above it, rather than a plain box. It is used automatically wherever one of those descriptions is edited; there is nothing to open or navigate to on its own.

## Common tasks

- Format text as bold, italic, strikethrough or inline code.
- Add a heading, a bullet or numbered list, a quote, a code block or a divider.
- Insert or remove a link, or upload and insert an image where images are allowed.
- Switch to "Edit HTML source" to see or change the underlying markup directly.
- Undo or redo your last changes.

## Good to know

Switching this app off does not remove the field — the same description still saves, just as a plain text box without the toolbar.
