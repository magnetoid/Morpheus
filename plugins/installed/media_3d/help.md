## What it's for
This app is meant to add a 3D or augmented-reality preview, and a shoppable video with tappable product cards, to a product's page. Right now neither shows anywhere on your storefront: there is no screen to attach a 3D model or a shoppable video to a product, so shoppers never see this feature.

## Good to know
A settings panel offers a maximum file size for the 3D model and a maximum number of cards on a shoppable video, but changing either has no effect yet, because nothing on the storefront reads the value you set.
