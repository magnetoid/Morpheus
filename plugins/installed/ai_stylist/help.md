## What it's for
This app is meant to give shoppers their own AI chat on the storefront — a separate, shopper-facing assistant from Linda, who talks only to your team. Today it adds the settings below, but it does not show anything to a shopper: no chat button or window appears on the storefront yet.

## Set it up
There is nothing useful to set up yet. The fields in [](settings:ai_stylist) are saved when you fill them in, but they have no visible effect until a storefront chat is built to use them.

## Good to know
Setting a name, a welcome message, or requiring consent here does not change anything a shopper sees right now — the storefront side of this app has not been built. It shares some of the same AI connection as Linda, but it is not Linda, and switching these settings does not affect her chat in the dashboard at all.
