## What it's for
This app is meant to add two product rails: "Trending with shoppers like you" on the cart page, and "Looks like you" on checkout, both built from what shoppers buy and look at together. Right now neither ever shows a product — the storefront does not feed either rail any data, so the sections never appear.

## Good to know
A settings panel offers one switch for requiring a shopper's consent before showing these rails, but since the rails never show a product, it currently has no effect either.
