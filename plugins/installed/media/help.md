## What it's for

This app gives you one place to browse every image, video, audio file, PDF, spreadsheet and document in your store, together with your product photos and digital download files, grouped into tabs by type. Staff use it to find a file, check or edit its alt text, tags and description, delete one they uploaded here, or pick a file for another part of the dashboard.

## Common tasks

- Browse or search the [Media](page:library) library by type, filename or tag.
- Upload a new image, video, audio file or document.
- Edit the title, alt text, description or tags on a file you uploaded here.
- Delete a file you uploaded here.
- Ask Linda to search the media library for a file.

## Good to know

Product photos and digital download files show up here too, but you can only edit their alt text — editing or deleting them fully is done from the product itself. On a store with a very large number of files, older items in a tab can be left out with no indication, since each tab only loads a limited number at a time; there is currently no page-by-page browsing to reach the rest.
