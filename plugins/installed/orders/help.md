## What it's for

This app runs the cart, checkout and an order's whole life: payment, shipping, cancelling and refunds. Staff manage orders from [Orders](section:orders); shoppers see theirs in their account.

## Set it up

[Checkout, cart & invoices](settings:orders) sets how long an unpaid order holds its stock, whether digital-only carts skip the shipping step, and whether buying digital content asks the customer to waive their right of withdrawal, as EU law expects. It sets whether guests can check out, and what checkout asks besides the address: a note, a gift message, terms to accept. Add your business name, address and VAT number there for invoices. It also sets whether a cancel pays a paid order back, how cash-on-delivery and bank-transfer refunds are paid, whether customers get an email per parcel or one at the end, and whether the last parcel marks an order shipped or fulfilled.

## Common tasks

- See, search and update orders.
- Ship an order in one parcel or several, each with tracking, or cancel it before any parcel leaves.
- Add staff-only notes.
- Print an invoice, or a packing slip per shipment, with no prices.
- Refund in full or in part: to the card, to store credit, or as already paid back.
- Approve or reject returns.
- Ask Linda to search, ship, cancel or refund orders, or review a return; she asks for your yes before any change.

## Good to know

An order with nothing to pay (a free download, a cart a gift card covers) is paid when placed. An unpaid order is cancelled after the time you set (a payment link: its hours), freeing its stock, gift card, points or store credit; zero turns this off. Cancelling releases a held card and restocks; once a parcel has gone, refund what did not ship instead. A withdrawal from the contract ([Returns portal](app:returns_portal)) cancels and pays back an order none of which has left, whatever the cancel settings; otherwise it opens a return. A customer's past guest orders join their account once their email is verified, never just by registering. Sales count orders placed and not cancelled, after refunds: one left unpaid at checkout does not count, cash on delivery and a held card do. The shopper's note and gift message print on the packing slip; the order keeps when its terms were accepted. Without guest checkout, a shopper signs in first and keeps their cart. Invoices list each tax rate separately.
