## What it's for

This app runs the shopping cart, checkout and everything that happens to an order afterwards: payment, shipping, cancelling and refunds. Staff manage every order from [Orders](section:orders); shoppers see their own orders from their account.

## Set it up

The defaults work for a new store. Open [Checkout, cart & invoices](settings:orders) to change how long an unpaid order holds its stock before it is cancelled automatically, whether digital-only carts skip the shipping step, and whether buying digital content requires the customer to first agree to lose their right to a change of mind, as EU law expects. Add your business name, address and VAT or tax number there too, so they print on every invoice.

## Common tasks

- See, search, and update the status of orders.
- Ship an order in one parcel or several, each with its tracking number and its own email to the customer, or cancel it.
- Add a staff-only note to an order.
- Print an order's invoice, or a packing slip for each shipment: what goes in the box and where, with no prices.
- Refund an order, in full or in part.
- Approve or reject a return request.
- Ask Linda to search orders, ship some or all of an order's items, cancel one, refund one, or review a return — she asks for your yes before she changes anything. She can also give you the links to an order's invoice and packing slip.

## Good to know

An order with nothing to pay (a free download, or a cart a gift card covers in full) is marked paid as it is placed: the customer goes straight to the confirmation page and gets any download link at once, without a payment step. An order that is never paid is cancelled automatically after the time you set, which frees its stock and returns any gift card, loyalty, or store-credit payment used on it; set the time to zero to turn this off and handle it by hand. A customer's past guest orders are only added to their account once their email is verified, never just by registering with it. Signing in merges whatever was already in the cart into the customer's own cart. Invoices and packing slips print from the browser; choose Save as PDF there to keep a copy. An invoice lists each tax rate separately for orders placed from this version on; older orders show one tax line.
