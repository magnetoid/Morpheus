## What it's for

This app runs the shopping cart, checkout and everything that happens to an order afterwards: payment, shipping, cancelling and refunds. Staff manage every order from [Orders](section:orders); shoppers see their own orders from their account.

## Set it up

The defaults work for a new store. [Checkout, cart & invoices](settings:orders) sets how long an unpaid order holds its stock before it is cancelled, whether digital-only carts skip the shipping step, and whether buying digital content first asks the customer to give up their right to a change of mind, as EU law expects. Add your business name, address and VAT or tax number there for every invoice. It also sets how a cash-on-delivery or bank-transfer refund is paid back by default, whether customers get an email per parcel or one at the end, and whether the last parcel marks an order shipped or only fulfilled.

## Common tasks

- See, search, and update the status of orders.
- Ship an order in one parcel or several, each with its tracking number, or cancel it.
- Add a staff-only note to an order.
- Print an order's invoice, or a packing slip for each shipment: what goes in the box and where, with no prices.
- Refund an order in full or in part, to the card, to store credit, or as already paid back.
- Approve or reject a return request.
- Ask Linda to search, ship, cancel or refund orders, or review a return; she asks for your yes before changing anything, and can give you an order's invoice and packing slip links.

## Good to know

An order with nothing to pay (a free download, or a cart a gift card covers) is marked paid as it is placed, with no payment step. An unpaid order is cancelled after the time you set, freeing its stock and any gift card, points or store credit; zero turns this off. A customer's past guest orders join their account once their email is verified, never just by registering. Sales are one figure everywhere, a contact's spend and Linda's answers included: orders placed and not cancelled, after their refunds. One left unpaid at checkout does not count; cash on delivery and a held card do. Invoices and packing slips print from the browser, or save as PDF. An invoice lists each tax rate separately for orders placed from this version on; older orders show one tax line.
