## What it's for

This app is the backbone of selling: it runs the shopping cart, checkout, and everything that happens to an order afterwards — confirming payment, shipping it, cancelling it, and refunding it. Staff manage every order from [Orders](section:orders); shoppers see their own orders from their account.

## Set it up

The defaults work for a new store. Open [Checkout & cart](settings:orders) to change how long an unpaid order holds its stock before it is cancelled automatically, whether digital-only carts skip the shipping step, and whether buying digital content requires the customer to first agree to lose their right to a change of mind, as EU law expects.

## Common tasks

- See, search, and update the status of orders.
- Ship an order and add tracking, or cancel one.
- Add a staff-only note to an order.
- Refund an order, in full or in part.
- Approve or reject a return request.
- Ask Linda to search orders, cancel one, refund one, or review a return — she asks for your yes before she changes anything.

## Good to know

An order that is never paid is cancelled automatically after the time you set, which frees its stock and returns any gift card, loyalty, or store-credit payment used on it; set the time to zero to turn this off and handle it by hand. A customer's past guest orders are only added to their account once their email is verified, never just by registering with it. Signing in merges whatever was already in the cart into the customer's own cart.
