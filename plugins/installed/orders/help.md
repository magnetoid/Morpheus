## What it's for

This app runs the cart, checkout and everything that happens to an order afterwards: payment, shipping, cancelling and refunds. Staff manage orders from [Orders](section:orders); shoppers see theirs in their account.

## Set it up

The defaults work for a new store. [Checkout, cart & invoices](settings:orders) sets how long an unpaid order holds its stock, whether digital-only carts skip the shipping step, and whether buying digital content first asks the customer to give up their right to a change of mind, as EU law expects. It sets whether guests can check out, and what checkout asks besides the address: a note, a gift message, terms to accept. Add your business name, address and VAT number there for invoices. It also sets how a cash-on-delivery or bank-transfer refund is paid back, whether customers get an email per parcel or one at the end, and whether the last parcel marks an order shipped or fulfilled.

## Common tasks

- See, search, and update the status of orders.
- Ship an order in one parcel or several, each with its tracking number, or cancel it.
- Add a staff-only note to an order.
- Print an order's invoice, or a packing slip for each shipment: what goes in the box and where, with no prices.
- Refund an order in full or in part, to the card, to store credit, or as already paid back.
- Approve or reject a return request.
- Ask Linda to search, ship, cancel or refund orders, or review a return; she asks for your yes before changing anything.

## Good to know

An order with nothing to pay (a free download, a cart a gift card covers) is paid as it is placed. An unpaid order is cancelled after the time you set, freeing its stock, gift card, points or store credit; zero turns this off. A customer's past guest orders join their account once their email is verified, never just by registering. Sales are one figure everywhere, a contact's spend and Linda's answers included: orders placed and not cancelled, after their refunds. One left unpaid at checkout does not count; cash on delivery and a held card do. The shopper's note and gift message print on the packing slip; the order keeps when its terms were accepted. While guests cannot check out, a shopper signs in first and keeps their cart. Invoices list each tax rate separately.
