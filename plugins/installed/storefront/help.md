## What it's for
This app is your storefront: the home page, browsing by product and category, search, the cart, checkout, order confirmation, and each customer's own account area for past orders, returns, cancellations, addresses, saved payment methods and store credit. It also carries your journal, a vendor directory and a marketplace landing page if you sell through vendors, and a handful of standard pages — About, Contact, Shipping and Returns. Shoppers use it to browse and buy; its content comes from your catalogue, your orders and your customers, and its look from your active theme.

## Set it up
There is nothing to turn on — it runs as soon as your store has active products and a theme. The name shown throughout the storefront comes from your store's name setting.

## Common tasks
- Browse your own storefront to see what a shopper sees, before a sale or a new theme goes live.
- Write your own Shipping and Returns page from [Pages](app:cms) — it replaces the generic one shown at those addresses.
- Write an introduction for the Products, Vendors or Journal listing pages, also from Pages.
- Point a customer to their account page for their order history, a return, or to cancel an order before it ships.

## Good to know
Home, Cart, Checkout, My account and Contact are built into this app, not pages you create yourself — they're listed for reference in Pages. A message sent through the Contact page is saved as a lead when you have [the CRM app](app:crm) turned on. A customer can cancel their own order themselves only before it is dispatched, and a paid order is refunded the same way any other refund is.
