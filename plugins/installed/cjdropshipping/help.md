## What it's for

This app connects your store to CJdropshipping, a supplier with its own open programme and warehouses worldwide. Once connected, staff can search CJ's catalogue from the dashboard, bring a product in as a draft to review and price, and let the store send, pay, and track supplier orders on its own.

## Set it up

Create an API key in your CJ account (My CJ → Authorization → API) and paste it on [CJdropshipping](settings:cjdropshipping), along with the warehouse country to ship from and a price markup. CJ prices in US dollars, so also set the exchange rate to your store's currency if it is not USD. From the [CJdropshipping](page:cj) page you can also register CJ's own tracking alerts, so updates arrive the moment CJ ships rather than only on the periodic refresh.

## Common tasks

- Test the connection and see the CJ account's balance.
- Register tracking alerts so CJ can push updates directly.
- Search CJ and import a product as a draft, then review its price before publishing it.
- Link an existing product to a CJ item on its edit page.
- Send or pay an order by hand if needed, or retry one that failed.

## Good to know

A paid order is sent to CJ automatically, and can be paid straight from your CJ balance if you choose. Tracking normally arrives the moment CJ ships, with a periodic refresh as a backstop. If a store order is cancelled after being sent to CJ, CJ cancels it too only while it is still in an early enough state; otherwise this only flags it for staff, who will need to cancel it directly with CJ.
