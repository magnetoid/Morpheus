## What it's for

This app creates coupon codes that a shopper types in at checkout: a percentage off, a fixed amount off, free shipping, or buy X get Y. A coupon can have a minimum order size, a limit on how many times it can be used in total and per customer, a start and end date, and can apply to specific products, categories or collections only, with or without items that are already on sale.

## Common tasks

- Add a new coupon code, its discount, and its limits, from [Coupons](page:coupons).
- Limit a coupon to products (by SKU or slug), categories, or collections under Where it applies. A category covers the categories below it.
- Turn a coupon off without deleting it, or delete it entirely.
- Ask Linda to list, create or change a coupon. She shows you the change and waits for your yes.
- See which coupons are usable right now on the [Marketing](section:marketing) overview.

## Good to know

A limited coupon discounts only the lines it covers, and the code box refuses it, with a reason, when nothing in the cart is covered or the code has expired. For buy X get Y, every set of X items lets the cheapest Y items in the cart get the coupon's value as a percent off (100 means free). Each coupon keeps a running count of how many times it has been used, free shipping codes included. Automatic discounts that apply without a code are the separate [Promotions](app:promotions) app.
