## What it's for

This app creates coupon codes: a percentage off, a fixed amount off, or free shipping, that a shopper types in at checkout. A coupon can have a minimum order size, a limit on how many times it can be used in total and per customer, a start and end date, and apply to specific products, categories, or collections only.

## Common tasks

- Add a new coupon code, its discount, and its limits, from [Coupons](page:coupons).
- Turn a coupon off without deleting it, or delete it entirely.
- Limit a coupon to specific products, categories, or collections.
- See which coupons are usable right now on the [Marketing](section:marketing) overview.

## Good to know

A coupon only discounts an order when the shopper enters its code; automatic discounts that apply without a code — such as 10% off orders over a set amount — are the separate [Promotions](app:promotions) app. Each coupon keeps a running count of how many times it has been used.
