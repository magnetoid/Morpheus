## What it's for

This app defines which countries and regions you ship to and what shipping costs in each one. The cost it works out is added to the order total automatically at checkout. Once you connect a carrier's key, it can quote a live rate from that carrier instead of a flat cost.

## Set it up

Add at least one [shipping zone](page:zones) with the countries or regions it covers, then add a flat-rate cost to it. To quote live rates from Shippo or EasyPost instead, add that carrier's key and default origin address on the same page.

## Common tasks

- Add or edit a shipping zone and its countries from the [Shipping zones](page:zones) page.
- Set a flat shipping cost for a zone.
- Connect a Shippo or EasyPost key to quote live carrier rates.
- Ask Linda to list your shipping zones or add a flat-rate option.

## Good to know

Only flat-rate and carrier-quoted costs can be fully set up from this page today. Weight-tiered, order-total-tiered and free-over-a-threshold rates are offered as options, but the amounts and thresholds they need can't be entered here yet, so a rate created that way won't charge correctly or show the free-shipping progress message in the cart. The "Apply tax to shipping cost" checkbox does not currently change whether tax is charged on shipping. New rates are priced in US dollars.

Your store's shipping page lists the active rates with what each one costs, its delivery time and its free-shipping threshold, taken from here. With no rate set up, the page says that checkout shows the delivery options and their cost for the shopper's address, so it never promises a price checkout won't charge.
