## What it's for

This app issues gift cards, keeps a running record of every balance change, and lets a shopper apply a card's code at checkout toward their order. You can issue a card by hand for any amount, or sell gift cards on the storefront as a product. Linda can also issue a card or look one up for you.

## Set it up

To sell gift cards on the storefront, create a product whose SKU is one of the values in [](field:gift_cards.sellable_skus) under [Gift cards](settings:gift_cards) settings — the default is GIFT-CARD. Once an order containing that product is paid, a card is issued automatically for each unit and emailed to the buyer; the email's wording can be edited from the store's email template list.

## Common tasks

- Issue a gift card to a customer, for any amount, from [Gift cards](page:gift_cards).
- Look up a card's balance and history by its code.
- Disable or reactivate a card.
- Ask Linda to issue a card or check a balance.

## Good to know

A shopper's card pays down whatever tax and shipping leave owing; any amount left over stays on the card for later. Cancelling an order puts a spent card's balance back; a refund returns the matching share of what the card paid. Codes are 16 characters, letters and digits only, with no easily-confused characters.
