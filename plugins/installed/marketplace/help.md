## What it's for

Vendors apply to sell through your store. Once approved, each gets a self-service area to manage their own products, see and fulfil their own orders, and track what they are owed. When a shopper's order includes items from more than one vendor, it is split into a separate order per vendor, with your commission taken out automatically.

## Set it up

[Marketplace](settings:marketplace) settings hold the defaults a new vendor starts from: [](field:marketplace.default_commission_percent) and [](field:marketplace.min_payout_threshold). Either can be changed for one vendor afterwards from their own page.

## Common tasks

- Review and approve or reject vendor applications on [Applications](page:applications).
- See every vendor, and change one vendor's commission rate, on [Vendors](page:vendors).
- See open vendor orders on [Vendor orders](page:orders).
- Run a payout and mark payouts as paid on [Payouts](page:payouts).
- Check a vendor's payout account details on [Payout accounts](page:payout-accounts).
- See sales and payout totals on [Reports](page:reports).

## Good to know

A vendor only ever sees their own products and orders. Commission is worked out and credited to the vendor automatically the moment an order is placed — there is nothing to run by hand. A payout only generates once a vendor has earned at least the minimum threshold.
