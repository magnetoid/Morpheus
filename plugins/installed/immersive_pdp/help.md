## What it's for

This app adds a sticky buy bar to a product page, so the price and an add-to-cart button stay visible as a shopper scrolls, on both phone and desktop. When a product has more than one edition or option, the bar includes a picker so a shopper can choose one without scrolling back up.

## Set it up

[](field:immersive_pdp.sticky_buybox) in [](settings:immersive_pdp) turns the sticky bar on or off.

## Good to know

The bar's option picker only appears when a product has more than one active variant; a product with just one, or none, shows the bar without it, and a product with no variants at all shows no bar. Turning this app off removes the bar from every product page.
