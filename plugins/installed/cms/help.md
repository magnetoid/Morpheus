## What it's for

This app builds the extra pages on your storefront that aren't products — an About page, a FAQ, journal articles and similar — plus the menus that link to them, small reusable content blocks a theme can place in several spots, and forms that save every submission. Staff write and publish pages and menus from the dashboard.

## Common tasks

- Write, edit, schedule or publish a page from the [Pages](page:pages) list.
- Build or reorder a navigation menu, including a dropdown panel of your categories, topics or authors, from the [Menus](page:menus) list.
- Check recent form submissions on the [Forms](page:forms) list.
- Ask Linda to create or update a content block, or to publish or unpublish a page.

## Good to know

Blocks have no "add new" button on their dashboard page — today you can only create or change one by asking Linda. Forms can't be created from anywhere in the dashboard at all; the [Forms](page:forms) list only shows forms that already exist and their submissions. A form submission that includes an email address becomes a lead in your CRM automatically, if you have that app on. Journal-style pages get extra treatment: a reading-time estimate, and FAQ search markup when the page has a clearly labelled FAQ section. Turning this app off takes down every page, menu and form it manages.
