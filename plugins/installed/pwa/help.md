## What it's for
This app lets a shopper install your storefront like an app on their phone or computer, and keeps a branded page ready to show if their connection drops while browsing. It also keeps a copy of pages and images a visitor has already loaded, so a return visit can be faster.

## Set it up
In [](settings:pwa) you can turn the offline page on or off with [](field:pwa.offline_enabled), and set the colour shown in the browser's own bar when your store is installed with [](field:pwa.theme_color).

## Good to know
Signing in, your cart, checkout, a customer's account and the dashboard are never kept for offline use, so nobody ever sees stale cart or order information. Your store's name and icon come from what you've already set elsewhere.
