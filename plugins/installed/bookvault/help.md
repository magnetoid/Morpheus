## What it's for

This app connects your store to Bookvault, a print-on-demand book fulfilment service. Once connected, a paid order containing a linked book is sent to Bookvault automatically, and you can see every order you've sent and resend one if needed. It can also quote Bookvault's shipping cost at checkout for books it ships (titles with a 13-character ISBN as their SKU), as one of your shipping app's rate options.

## Set it up

Connect your Bookvault account from the [Bookvault](page:overview) page — it asks Bookvault for a client token and store ID and saves them. Turn [](field:bookvault.auto_send_on_paid) off if you'd rather send orders to Bookvault yourself instead of automatically.

## Common tasks

- Connect or disconnect your Bookvault account from the [Bookvault](page:overview) page.
- Link a product to its Bookvault edition, and send or resend it from the products list.
- Check which orders have been sent to Bookvault and when, from the [Bookvault](page:overview) page.

## Good to know

A product must be linked to a Bookvault edition before an order containing it is sent. Turning this app off removes its product-list column and product-page card, and stops sending orders automatically.
