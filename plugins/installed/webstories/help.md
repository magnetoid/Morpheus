## What it's for
This app turns a product's own photos and description into a short, tappable visual story — the format Google calls Web Stories — and shows a card linking to it on the product's page. It builds the story the moment a product with a photo is saved, and rebuilds it whenever the product or its photos change, so there is nothing to write yourself.

## Set it up
Nothing to configure before it works — a story builds itself the first time a product with a photo is saved.

## Common tasks
- Turn the story preview card off a product's page with [](field:webstories.show_pdp_embed); the story keeps its own address either way.
- Open a product's story yourself, the way a shopper would, to see what it looks like.

## Good to know
A story needs at least one product photo to build at all, and needs a handful of pages before search engines may index it — a shorter story still plays, it just stays out of search. There is no way to write or edit a story's own words; it is always rebuilt from the product's own name, photos and description.
