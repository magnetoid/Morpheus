## What it's for

This app adds a bulk-reorder option for your signed-in customers: instead of adding products to the cart one at a time, they can paste or upload a list of SKUs and quantities and have them all added to the cart at once, at your regular catalog prices.

## Common tasks

- Point a wholesale or repeat customer to your store's bulk-reorder page so they can reorder many items in one paste.

## Good to know

This app also has price lists, quotes, and net-payment terms for business accounts, built from the [Price lists](page:pricelists) page and Linda. None of these currently change what a customer is charged or how an order is invoiced — a price list isn't yet linked to any account, and quotes and payment terms have no effect at checkout today.
