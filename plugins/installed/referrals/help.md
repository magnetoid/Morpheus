## What it's for

This app is meant to let a customer share a personal link so that, when a friend places their first qualifying order, both the customer and their friend get store credit — with an optional contest on top for whoever refers the most people. None of that exists yet: turning the app on does not create a link for anyone to share, does not record who referred whom, and does not add credit to anyone's account.

## Good to know

The app ships switched off. Turning it on adds an empty space to the account page and to checkout; neither shows anything useful today. The Referrals card on the [Customers](section:customers) page will always read zero.
