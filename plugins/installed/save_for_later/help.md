## What it's for
This app is meant to let a shopper move an item out of their cart into a saved list without losing it, and later tell them if its price drops or it comes back in stock.

## Good to know
This app ships switched off, and switching it on does not change anything today: the button it adds to the cart has nothing behind it, and nothing checks prices or stock for a saved item.
