## What it's for

This app changes the order products appear in across grids and lists on the storefront — the home page, category and shop pages, search results, and an author's page — so a particular shopper sees the items they are most likely to buy nearer the top. It works out likely interest from what that shopper has recently looked at and from what customers tend to buy together, recomputed every night. No product is added or removed, only reordered.

## Good to know

This runs automatically — there is no screen in the dashboard to switch it on, change its ranking, or see what it is doing. A shopper who has not accepted functional or analytics cookies sees the plain, unpersonalised order. There is no separate "frequently bought together" box shown on a product page today; the effect is the order of existing lists only.
