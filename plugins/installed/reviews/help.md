## What it's for

Shoppers can write a star rating and a review on a product page. A review from someone who actually bought the product is published right away; anyone else's review waits for a staff member to approve it. Staff moderate every review from one page in the dashboard.

Under a product's price, the app also shows up to three signals: the star rating with its review count, the share of reviews left by verified buyers, and a line such as "12 ordered in the last 3 days." Each one shows only once there is enough real data behind it.

## Set it up

Choose which signals show, and how much data each one needs first, in [](settings:reviews). By default all three are on, the rating needs at least 3 reviews, and the purchases line needs at least 3 orders in the last 72 hours.

## Common tasks

- Approve or hide a review from the [Reviews](page:reviews) page, filtered by status or by a low star rating.
- Ask Linda to list reviews waiting for approval, or to approve one.
- Ask Linda to change one of the settings above, for example to hide the purchases line.

## Good to know

Only approved reviews count toward a product's star rating and review count, and only approved reviews show on the product page. The verified-buyer share also needs at least 5 approved reviews, and shows only when at least half of them are from buyers. A moderation decision can take up to ten minutes to show up on the storefront, because product pages and these numbers are cached for a short time. You can also save a written reply to a review from the moderation page, but it isn't shown anywhere yet. Turning this app off stops shoppers from writing new reviews and removes the signals under the price, but existing reviews and ratings keep showing on the storefront.
