## What it's for

Shoppers can write a star rating and a review on a product page. A review from someone who actually bought the product is published right away; anyone else's review waits for a staff member to approve it. Staff moderate every review from one page in the dashboard.

## Common tasks

- Approve or hide a review from the [Reviews](page:reviews) page, filtered by status or by a low star rating.
- Ask Linda to list reviews waiting for approval, or to approve one.

## Good to know

Only approved reviews count toward a product's star rating and review count, and only approved reviews show on the product page. A moderation decision can take up to ten minutes to show up on the storefront, because product pages are cached for a short time. You can also save a written reply to a review from the moderation page, but it isn't shown anywhere yet. Turning this app off stops shoppers from writing new reviews, but existing reviews and ratings keep showing on the storefront.
