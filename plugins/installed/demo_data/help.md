## What it's for
This fills the store with sample data for trying things out: a full bookstore demo with categories, products, customers and orders, or a batch of randomly generated products themed to match your storefront.

## Set it up
Nothing to switch on; everything here runs on demand from one page.

## Common tasks
- Generate a batch of random demo products, themed to your storefront, in [](page:index).
- Load the full sample bookstore — categories, collections, vendors, customers and orders — from the same page.
- Wipe the sample bookstore and start it over, using the "wipe demo first" option before loading it again.
- Remove only the randomly generated demo products, leaving your real products and the bookstore sample untouched.

## Good to know
Generating random products is themed automatically from your active storefront design — bookstore, apparel, or a general theme — unless you type in your own. Running the same action again does not pile up duplicates: it reuses what it already created and only adds what is missing. This is meant for trying the store out, not for real catalog data.
