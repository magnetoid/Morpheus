How we measure it

For each book we estimate the paper mass from its recorded weight and size, then derive the wood used (about {{ wood_factor }} kg of wood per kg of paper) and the carbon of producing it (about {{ co2_per_kg_paper }} kg CO2 per kg of paper, plus printing and binding). These are estimates, sourced from published paper-industry figures — not a precise per-copy measurement.

Planting a tree

At checkout you can add {{ tree_price|money }} to plant a tree. A maturing tree sequesters roughly {{ kg_co2_per_tree|floatformat:0 }} kg of CO2 a year — comfortably more than a typical book order’s footprint. We pool these contributions and direct them to reforestation. It’s always optional, and never added without your choice.
