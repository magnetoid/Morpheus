## What it's for

On a book's product page, this app shows an estimated production footprint — paper, wood, and CO₂ — worked out from the book's physical details. At checkout, a shopper can opt into a flat "plant a tree" add-on; pledges build into a running total you fulfil yourself, since no outside planting service is connected. A public page and a footer link show the store's running totals.

## Set it up

From [Eco impact](settings:eco_impact) settings, turn the footprint badge on product pages on or off ([](field:eco_impact.show_on_pdp)) and set the add-on's price ([](field:eco_impact.tree_price)). The CO₂, wood, and paper figures used in the estimate can also be tuned there ([](field:eco_impact.kg_co2_per_tree), [](field:eco_impact.wood_factor), [](field:eco_impact.co2_per_kg_paper)) if you have better supplier numbers.

## Common tasks

- Turn the footprint badge on product pages on or off.
- Change how much the tree add-on costs at checkout.
- See how many trees shoppers have pledged and how many orders contributed, on [Eco impact](page:eco_impact).

## Good to know

The badge only shows for book products with a recorded weight, page count, or dimensions — a product without that data shows nothing. The footprint figures are estimates, shown as such. Cancelling an order removes its pledge from the running total.
