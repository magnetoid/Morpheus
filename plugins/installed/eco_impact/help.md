## What it's for

On a book's product page, this app shows an estimated production footprint — paper, wood, and CO₂ — worked out from the book's physical details. At checkout, a shopper can add a flat tree-planting pledge; pledges build into a running total you fulfil yourself, since no outside planting service is connected. A public page and a footer link ("Book footprint") show how the estimate is made and how many trees were pledged.

## Set it up

From [Eco impact](settings:eco_impact) settings, turn the footprint badge on product pages on or off ([](field:eco_impact.show_on_pdp)) and set the add-on's price ([](field:eco_impact.tree_price)). The CO₂, wood, and paper figures used in the estimate can also be tuned there ([](field:eco_impact.kg_co2_per_tree), [](field:eco_impact.wood_factor), [](field:eco_impact.co2_per_kg_paper)) if you have better supplier numbers.

## Common tasks

- Turn the footprint badge on product pages on or off.
- Change how much the tree pledge costs at checkout.
- See how many trees shoppers have pledged and how many orders contributed, on [Eco impact](page:eco_impact).

## Good to know

The badge only shows for book products with a recorded weight, page count, or dimensions — a product without that data shows nothing. The footprint figures are estimates, shown as such, with a link to how they are made. Cancelling an order removes its pledge from the running total.

The shop never calls a pledge an offset, and never says an order or the store is climate neutral: since 27 September 2026 EU consumer law bans climate claims based on offsetting and generic environmental claims. The figure for what the pledged trees may absorb appears only on your dashboard, as an estimate. The [SEO](app:seo) site scan lists any such claim elsewhere in your copy.
