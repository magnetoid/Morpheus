## What it's for
This app builds the photo gallery on every product page: a slider through all of a product's photos at the top, with arrows and a thumbnail strip, and, further down the page, a second strip repeating its non-cover photos for a closer look. It needs no setup — it runs from the photos already on a product.

## Common tasks
- Add, remove, reorder or choose a product's cover photo from [](section:products); the gallery always follows that order.

## Good to know
The top slider keeps the shape set in [](field:catalog.image_aspect_ratio) — square, portrait, or whatever your store uses; the second strip further down is always square, whatever that setting is. The second strip never appears for a product with only one photo, and a product with no photo yet shows your store's placeholder image instead.
