Renders every product image (capped at 15) as a horizontal slider with arrows, thumbnail strip, and keyboard navigation. Slot context: images — list of image dicts from the GraphQL PRODUCT_DETAIL_QUERY response. Each entry: {url, altText, isPrimary, sortOrder}. Sort order: by `sortOrder` ASC so slot 0 is always the first slide. Cap at 15 entries per the product-slider spec.
