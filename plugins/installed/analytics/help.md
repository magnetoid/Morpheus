## What it's for
Analytics tracks what happens on your storefront — sessions, page views, product views, searches, cart activity, checkouts and orders — and turns it into a traffic overview, a real-time view, a conversion funnel, customer retention cohorts, and ad attribution by channel. It also watches for unusual swings in your daily numbers and flags them. It's for anyone who wants to know how the store is actually performing, not just how much it sold.

## Set it up
Nothing to connect — tracking starts as soon as the app is on. On [Analytics](settings:analytics) you can choose how many days of raw events to keep, and whether to exclude staff visits (on by default, so your own browsing doesn't skew customer numbers) or all logged-in customers (off by default).

## Common tasks
- Check sessions, orders, revenue, a 7-day revenue forecast and recent agent activity on [Traffic](page:overview)
- Watch what's happening on the storefront right now on [Real-time](page:realtime)
- See where shoppers drop out of the buying journey on [Funnel](page:funnel)
- Check what share of customers from one week are still buying weeks later on [Cohorts](page:cohorts)
- Compare ad spend to revenue under different attribution models on [Attribution](page:attribution)
- Export the traffic summary and top products as a CSV or JSON file

## Good to know
A purchase, checkout start, sign-up or login is only ever recorded from the store's own server-side events, never from a message the browser sends, so a visitor can't inflate your numbers by forging them. Pageviews and other browsing events skip known crawlers. The daily numbers feed three tiles on the dashboard home (sessions, conversion rate, AI referrals) and a nightly check that flags a metric moving unusually far from its normal range.
