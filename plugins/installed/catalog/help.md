## What it's for

This is where your products live: their names, descriptions, prices, photos, variants (size, colour and so on), categories, collections and tags. Anyone who manages what you sell works here every day. It also owns the one setting that decides the shape, crop and file format of every product photo shown anywhere in the store — on a product page, in a list, or on the storefront.

## Set it up

Open [](settings:catalog) to choose the frame every product photo is shown in ([](field:catalog.image_aspect_ratio)), whether a photo fills that frame or sits inside it with margins ([](field:catalog.image_fit)), and the quality and file format generated for each upload ([](field:catalog.image_quality), [](field:catalog.default_image_format)). [](field:catalog.pdp_image_width) and [](field:catalog.pdp_image_height) set the size of the photo on a product's own page. [](field:catalog.enable_avif_variant) also generates a second, smaller file for browsers that support it. If you make or label what you sell, enter the brand in [](field:catalog.default_brand): product pages and every shopping feed name it, unless an app knows better (a book's publisher). With neither, they name no brand.

## Common tasks

- Add, edit, archive or delete a product, its photos and its variants, from [](section:products).
- See each product's categories, collections, type and date added in the product list, sort by date added, and hide columns with Columns (your browser remembers).
- Build a category tree; opening a category also lists every product in the categories beneath it.
- Group products into a collection to feature together on the storefront or in a homepage rail.
- Ask Linda to find a product, check its price or stock, or change its status or price.
- Ask Linda for a product's price history and the lowest price before its current sale.

## Good to know

A download priced 0 (a PDF, say) is free and says so; on a variant that ships, 0 means the product's price.

Every price change is recorded, however it was made. When a product is on sale (its compare-at price is higher than its price), its page shows the lowest price it had in the 30 days before the reduction, as EU law requires; the line appears only when the recorded prices prove it. 

Your active top-level categories and their subcategories build the storefront's main navigation menu automatically. Switching a category off removes it from the menu along with everything beneath it. Shoppers whose browser cannot show the extra image format get the default one. A variant's size, and a variant attribute named colour, material or pattern, tell search engines how the variants differ.
