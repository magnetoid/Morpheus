## What it's for

This is where your products live: their names, descriptions, prices, photos, variants (size, colour and so on), categories, collections and tags. Anyone who manages what you sell works here every day. It also owns the one setting that decides the shape, crop and file format of every product photo shown anywhere in the store — on a product page, in a list, or on the storefront.

## Set it up

Open [](settings:catalog) to choose the frame every product photo is shown in ([](field:catalog.image_aspect_ratio)), whether a photo fills that frame or sits inside it with margins ([](field:catalog.image_fit)), and the quality and file format generated for each upload ([](field:catalog.image_quality), [](field:catalog.default_image_format)). [](field:catalog.pdp_image_width) and [](field:catalog.pdp_image_height) set the size of the photo on a product's own page. [](field:catalog.enable_avif_variant) also generates a second, smaller file for browsers that support it.

## Common tasks

- Add, edit, archive or delete a product, its photos and its variants, from [](section:products).
- Build a category tree; opening a category also lists every product in the categories beneath it.
- Group products into a collection to feature together on the storefront or in a homepage rail.
- Write a description for a tag so its tag page reads well, not just a bare product grid.
- Ask Linda to find a product, check its price or stock, or change its status or price.
- Ask Linda for a product's price history: every price it has had since the store began recording them, and the lowest price before its current sale.

## Good to know

Every price change is recorded, however it was made: on the product page here, by Linda, by a scheduled price or by an import. When a product is on sale (its compare-at price is higher than its price), its page shows the lowest price it had in the 30 days before the reduction, as EU law requires; the line appears only when the recorded prices prove it, so a sale that started before recording began, or a product sold at the same price since it was created, shows none. Recording began with the price each product had at its last save.

Your active top-level categories and their subcategories build the storefront's main navigation menu automatically. Switching a category off removes it from the menu along with everything beneath it. The extra image format takes slightly longer to process on upload; shoppers whose browser does not support it still get the default format, so nothing breaks for them.
