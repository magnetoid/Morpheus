## What it's for
Sales Channels gives you one place to see how every channel app (Google, Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, Microsoft, Amazon, Reddit, Snapchat, ChatGPT) is doing: whether it's connected, whether its product feed is live and how much of your catalog qualifies, whether its pixel is on, and its last 30 days of ad spend, clicks, conversions and revenue. It pulls all of this from the channel apps themselves — it has no settings or credentials of its own. Use it as the first stop before opening one channel's own page.

## Set it up
Nothing to configure here. The rows only fill in once you set up the channel apps themselves — connect an account, turn on a feed or pixel — from each one's own settings.

## Common tasks
- Check which channels are connected and which still need a feed or pixel turned on
- Open a channel's own page from the Open button on its row
- Refresh the connection and feed numbers with Refresh status
- Pull the latest 30-day ad spend, clicks, conversions and revenue with Refresh KPIs

## Good to know
Status rows and ad KPIs are cached, not live: a background job refreshes the status snapshot every 30 minutes and the ad KPIs once a day, so the page can lag by that much after you change something on a channel. Each channel's last 30 days of ad spend, conversions and platform-reported revenue (the cached KPIs above) also go to the [analytics](app:analytics) app's Attribution page, which is where spend is compared with the revenue your own store data credits to each channel. Turning this app off removes this overview and that spend feed (Attribution then shows revenue with the spend column blank) — each channel keeps its own page and keeps working.
