## What it's for

This is the dashboard you sign in to and work in every day. It is the frame the rest of Morpheus runs inside: a sidebar down the left, a search box and your account menu across the top, and the page in the middle where each part of the store shows its own screens. Every app you install adds its screens into this same shell instead of building its own.

The sidebar is organised into sections: [Home](section:home), [Linda](section:ai), [Orders](section:orders), [Products](section:products), [Customers](section:customers), [CRM](section:crm), [Vendors](section:vendors), [Affiliates](section:affiliates), [Marketing](section:marketing), [Channels](section:channels), [Content](section:content), [Analytics](section:analytics) and [SEO](section:seo). Home, Linda, Orders, Products, Customers and Analytics are always there; the rest appear only once an app that uses them is switched on. Open a section and its own pages show as tabs along the top — a section with only one page shows no tabs at all.

Settings, near the bottom of the sidebar, is where every app's configuration lives, grouped into categories such as Payments & checkout, Shipping & tax, Storefront, Marketing, AI, Team & security and Developer — a category is only listed once something is actually in it. The Apps catalogue, reached from the bottom of the Settings sidebar, lists every installed app, what it adds, and whether it is switched on, plus a Browse tab listing apps you have not installed yet with the command to install them.

## Common tasks

- Open a section from the sidebar, then its pages from the tabs at the top.
- Jump anywhere by clicking the search box (or pressing Cmd+K) and typing a page, order, product or customer.
- Open Settings and pick a category to change how an app behaves.
- Turn an app on or off, or see what it adds, from the Apps catalogue.
- Switch the dashboard between light, dark and system appearance from your account menu, top right.
- Ask Linda from her own section; she looks things up for you and asks for your yes before she changes anything.
- Work through the "Set up your store" checklist on the home page, or dismiss it once you're done.

## Good to know

A sidebar section appears only once an app contributes something to it, except Home, Linda, Orders, Products, Customers and Analytics, which always show. The same is true of Settings categories: General and Notifications always show; the rest appear only once an app has something there. This app is the dashboard shell itself, so it cannot be switched off.
