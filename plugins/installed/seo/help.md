## What it's for
SEO controls how products, categories and journal posts show up in search results and AI answers: titles and descriptions, structured data, the sitemap, redirects, a 404 monitor, which AI crawlers may read the store, and what to fix.

## Set it up
Fill in your organisation name, social profile links and search-engine verification codes, and choose the [](field:seo.organization_type) on [SEO](page:settings). Turn on [](field:seo.book_structured_data) if you sell books, and choose there which AI crawlers may read your pages. While preparing the store, turn on [](field:seo.hide_until_launch) to keep every page out of search, and switch it off on launch day. For Google's own numbers, paste a Search Console service-account key into [](field:seo.search_console_key); [Search performance](page:search-performance) lists the steps.

## Common tasks
- Fix titles and descriptions in bulk from [Bulk meta](page:bulk-meta), or one product at a time
- Re-run the [Audit](page:audit) to see which products score low and why
- Run the site scan on [Overview](page:overview): it reads every sitemap page and flags prices the page hides or the store no longer charges, wrong stock, broken images, wrong share-image sizes, links through redirects, and pages nothing links to
- Turn a 404 into a redirect, or dismiss it, from [404s](page:not-found)
- Add or edit a [redirect](page:redirects) by hand, or import a batch from a CSV file
- Check how many URLs the [Sitemap](page:sitemap) publishes, and rebuild or re-ping it
- Track a [keyword](page:keywords)'s Google position, clicks and impressions, updated nightly
- See which searches bring visitors from Google on [Search performance](page:search-performance)
- Fill in a title or description pattern once on [Templates](page:templates) instead of editing every product
- Add a FAQ, recipe, video or course to a page from its schema editor, which also lists blocks Google no longer shows, to delete

## Good to know
A title you typed always wins over a generated one. An empty organisation name, logo or e-mail falls back to your general store settings, and each edition of a product is described with its name, a photo and its barcode. Hidden until launch, the store stays out of search and AI crawlers; shoppers still browse it. Linda reads your SEO details, the last site scan, your [Index rules](page:rules) and title patterns freely; changing a title, a redirect, a schema block, a rule, a pattern or a setting, or starting a scan (one per ten minutes), needs your go-ahead first.

Files for AI crawlers list only categories with products and quote only approved reviews. The speed card shows real shoppers' page speed over 28 days.
