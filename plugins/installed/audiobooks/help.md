## What it's for

This app lets a book product also be sold as an audiobook. You add an audiobook edition, with its own price and SKU, on the book's product page, and shoppers get a "listen to a sample" player there. Buyers play and download the full recording from their Library. You can upload your own recording, or have the book's PDF read aloud by ElevenLabs, by ElevenLabs Studio, or by Qwen.

## Set it up

Choose the [](field:audiobooks.narration_provider) used when a book picks none, in [Settings](settings:audiobooks). For ElevenLabs and ElevenLabs Studio, fill in your [](field:audiobooks.elevenlabs_api_key), a [](field:audiobooks.elevenlabs_voice_id) and the [](field:audiobooks.elevenlabs_model). Qwen uses the Qwen key you saved under the AI settings; pick its [](field:audiobooks.qwen_voice) and [](field:audiobooks.qwen_model) here.

## Common tasks

- Turn on an audiobook edition for a book and set its price, SKU and narrator on the product page.
- Upload a finished recording, or a free sample clip.
- Pick a narration service and voice for one book, press "Make a sample" to hear the opening, then "Narrate the whole book".
- With ElevenLabs Studio, open the finished project in your ElevenLabs account to publish it to ElevenReader.
- Ask Linda where a book's narration stands, or to start one; she asks for your yes first, since narration is paid for.

## Good to know

Only the book itself is narrated: the PDF on the card, else the product's or the e-book edition's PDF. A scanned PDF has no text to read. The whole book takes an hour or more; the card shows how far it has got and why it stopped, and starting again carries on where it stopped as long as the service, voice and PDF stay the same. A ready edition keeps selling its current recording until a new one is finished. Nothing is narrated while AI use is paused for your store.
