## What it's for

This app lets a book product also be sold as an audiobook. You add an audiobook edition — its own price and SKU — from the book's product page, add a narrator name and an audio file or a short sample, and shoppers get a "listen to a sample" player on the product page. You can also generate the narration automatically from the book's text using ElevenLabs instead of uploading your own recording.

## Set it up

To generate narration automatically, fill in your [](field:audiobooks.elevenlabs_api_key), the [](field:audiobooks.elevenlabs_voice_id) to use, and the [](field:audiobooks.elevenlabs_model), in [Settings](settings:audiobooks).

## Common tasks

- Turn on an audiobook edition for a book and set its price, SKU and narrator from the product page.
- Upload a finished narration, or a free sample clip, for a book's audiobook edition.
- Generate narration from the book's own text (or a narration script you upload) with your connected ElevenLabs account.

## Good to know

An audiobook edition never needs shipping or stock tracking — it behaves like any other digital item at checkout. Generating narration does nothing while AI use is paused for your store.
