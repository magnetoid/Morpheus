## What it's for

This app is the record of every customer account and saved address, and the sign-in behind them. It also scores every customer who has placed at least one order on how recently, how often, and how much they have bought, and groups them into segments: Champions, Loyal, Potential, New, At risk, and Lost.

## Common tasks

- See how many customers are in each segment, and which ones moved recently, on [Segments](page:segments).
- Ask Linda to search for a customer, or look one up by email.

## Good to know

Segments are recomputed automatically every night; there is nothing to run by hand. A customer needs at least one order to be scored at all. A customer slipping into "At risk" can trigger a win-back email from the [Newsletter](app:newsletter) app.
