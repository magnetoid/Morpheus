## What it's for

This app is switched off by default and is not yet built. Today it only has a place to record whether a customer has agreed to hear from you by SMS, WhatsApp or push notification — nothing in the store reads that record or sends anything through it. Order emails, such as the one telling a customer their order has shipped, are the only messages sent after a purchase today.

## Good to know

Switching this app on from the Apps catalogue does not change anything a customer experiences today — there is nothing yet that writes to or reads from the record it keeps.
