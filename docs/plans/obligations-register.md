# Obligations register

Dated rules that may bind a store running Morpheus, kept beside the plans of record. The trends research of 2026-11-18 found that three EU rules arrived while the compliance work was recorded as finished; this register is what prevents that.

This is a register drawn from law-firm commentary and the published texts, **not legal advice**. Whether a rule binds a given store depends on where its seller is established and whom it sells to.

**How to keep it:**
- Add a row when a rule is announced.
- Change a row's position in the release that changes what Morpheus does.
- Re-read the rows due in the next three months when the plans are next reordered.

Every feature a row needs ships as an option inside Morpheus (owner, 2026-11-18), never as a fix on one store.

## Proposed, not yet law

- **Digital Fairness Act** (Commission proposal, 11 Nov 2026): a termination button for digital subscriptions, plus rules on urgency and personalised pricing. Design F6's cancel and any countdown block to be truthful now.
- **EU data omnibus** (Council mandate 21 Oct 2026, no Parliament position): cookie duties are unchanged until it passes. The consent app already gates every tag and server-side send, and reads Global Privacy Control as a refusal.

## Checked and not needed now

- **Digital Services Act:** micro and small platforms are exempt from the online-platform and marketplace duties, so the travel marketplaces need no trader-verification build while small. Showing who the host is remains a consumer-law duty.
- **Digital Product Passport:** no delegated act has been adopted for a consumer-goods product group yet.
- **EU Digital Identity Wallet** (member-state deadline 24 Dec 2026): no merchant duty yet.
