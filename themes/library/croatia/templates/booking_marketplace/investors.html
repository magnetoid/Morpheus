{% trans "02 · The opportunity" %}

{% trans "Experiences are the least digital part of travel" %}.

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Tours, activities and attractions are travel’s third-largest sector, worth $271 billion in 2025 and forecast to reach $342 billion by 2029. {% endblocktrans %}{% trans "Source" %} 6

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Yet only a third of experience bookings are made online, against almost two thirds for travel overall, and one operator in three has no online booking at all. {% endblocktrans %}{% trans "Source" %} 7

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Visitors book the flight and the hotel online, then book what they do after they land on the quay, at the hotel desk or not at all. That gap is the market we are building for. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "03 · The gap in Croatia" %}

{% trans "Demand is on the coast. Much of the best of the country is a day trip away." %}

1

{% trans "Visitors stay by the sea" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Most visitors stay on the coast and the islands. National parks, Istria’s hill towns, wineries and continental Croatia are reached on day trips, and a day trip needs a booking made before the visitor leaves the apartment. {% endblocktrans %}

2

{% trans "Small, owner-run hosts" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Skippers, kayak guides, family wineries, olive growers and cooking hosts sell much of what makes the country special. Many of them are hard to find online, and visitors can’t compare them. {% endblocktrans %}

3

{% trans "Global sites cover the famous few" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} International platforms list the best-known excursions, mostly in English. The long tail of local experiences, stays and products is thin, and few of them offer Croatian, the language of the country and of its many domestic and regional visitors. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "04 · What we built" %}

{% trans "One place for the whole trip" %}.

{% blocktrans trimmed %} croatia-experience.me covers what to do, where to stay and what to take home, across the whole country, on the same platform as Montenegro Experience. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Experiences, stays and local products" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}Tours and activities, hotels and stays, an events calendar, gift cards, and a shop of Croatian goods such as Istrian olive oil, Dalmatian wine, Pag cheese and lavender from Hvar.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Search that knows the country" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}One search for experiences, places and stays, by region, category and date. It understands typos and Croatian letters, typed with accents or without.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Reviewed local hosts" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}Every host applies to join, and the team reviews each application before the host’s listings go live.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Two languages, properly" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}Every page exists in English and in Croatian, and each is linked to the other so search engines show visitors the right one.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Sold where people shop" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}The catalogue feeds Google Shopping and the Meta catalogues behind Facebook and Instagram.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Ready for AI assistants" %}
