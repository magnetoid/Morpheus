A few honest notes

Where is my card actually stored?

On Stripe's servers, under their PCI-DSS Level 1 vault. We only keep a reference token (pm_…) — useless to anyone who isn't Stripe.

What happens when I click Remove?

We detach the card from your account immediately. Open orders aren't affected — they keep the charge that already went through.

Will you ever charge a saved card without me?

Only on a subscription renewal or a pre-authorised back-order — and only after you've explicitly opted in to that specific arrangement. Never out of the blue.
