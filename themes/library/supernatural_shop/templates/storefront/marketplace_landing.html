Who decides which shops join the marketplace?

We do, by hand. Each application is read at the desk. We look for shops with a clear voice and a list that lives somewhere — not on a spreadsheet of dropshipping.

How are orders shipped?

Each shop fulfills its own orders. If your basket pulls from three shops, you'll get three parcels — most arrive in a week or so.

What about returns?

Same return policy across every shop on the shelf. Thirty days, books in original condition, refund to the original card.

Is Supernatural Shop a publisher itself?

No. We're the shelf. The presses are the publishers. Each title here was made by one of them.
